FAQ

Common questions

Why 10%?

Because it includes the labor. Lower-percentage managers typically bill repair labor separately and charge retail for materials. Once those bills are added in, our true annual cost is usually lower. In our Alexandria example it was 28% lower.

What does "labor included" cover?

Routine repairs and maintenance our team performs: plumbing fixes, fixtures, doors and hardware, drywall patches, filter changes, seasonal tasks and similar work. You pay only for materials, at contractor pricing. Major replacements, capital improvements and work that needs a specialty trade are quoted separately and need your approval first.

Do you mark up contractor invoices?

No, never. We're already paid through our management fee, so when a job needs an outside specialist, you pay the contractor's actual price with no markup or coordination fee from us. Many managers add one, so it's worth asking anyone you're considering.

Do you only work with military and State Department owners?

No. Many of our owners are military or State Department families who appreciate a manager they can trust from overseas, but we work with any owner of a quality single-family home in our service area.

Do you manage short-term rentals?

We focus on long-term rentals. Stable, long-term tenants are better for the home and for your returns. If you're weighing options, tell us about your plans and we'll give you an honest take.

Do you work with portfolio owners?

Yes. Whether you own one home or several, you get the same all-inclusive model and one consolidated monthly statement.

How quickly do you respond?

We respond to inquiries within one business day, and most get a same-day reply.
